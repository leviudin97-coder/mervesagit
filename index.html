02 / Meet the artist

Many materials.
One curious mind.

I’m Merve Sagit, an interdisciplinary conceptual artist based in Dublin. My practice moves between photography, printmaking, textiles and fashion.

I explore cultural identity, memory and ecology through patient, material-led experimentation — from cyanotype and lumen printing to bio yarn and reclaimed materials.
